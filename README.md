# Smart Urban Planning Assistant

## Overview

The **Smart Urban Planning Assistant** is an AI-driven platform designed to empower urban planners, city officials, and individuals seeking relocation assistance with data-driven insights. By leveraging Neo4j for relationship-driven data representation and integrating advanced AI models, the assistant aims to enhance city development processes and facilitate informed decision-making.

## Features

### Dynamic Zone Analysis

- **Description:** Allows users to query relationships and data within specific zones, providing natural language summaries of zone details.

### Impact Analysis for Infrastructure Changes

- **Description:** Simulates the impact of proposed changes on interconnected zones, predicting effects on traffic, utility loads, and surrounding areas.

### Relocation Assistance

- **Description:** Assists individuals and families in evaluating potential relocation destinations based on personalized preferences and requirements.

  - **Scope:**
    - **User Profile Integration:** Incorporate user-specific data, such as age, family size, profession, income level, and preferences, to tailor recommendations.
    - **Location Analysis:** Assess various locations based on factors like amenities, housing prices, crime rates, walkability, transit options, green spaces, school quality, and healthcare facilities.
    - **Budget Alignment:** Ensure that recommended locations align with the user's budget constraints.
    - **Timeline Consideration:** Factor in the user's moving timeline to suggest locations with available housing and amenities.
    - **Relocation Support:** Provide information on moving services, housing assistance, and local community resources.

### School and Healthcare Facility Evaluation

- **Description:** Assists users in evaluating the quality of schools and healthcare facilities in potential relocation areas.

  - **Scope:**
    - **Data Integration:** Collect data on school ratings, healthcare facility ratings, and available services.
    - **Evaluation Tool:** Create a tool that allows users to assess and compare the quality of educational and healthcare institutions in different locations.

### Agentic Task Planning

- **Description:** Utilizes agent workflows for AI to access and process graph data, automatically pulling relevant data sources for detailed queries.

### Search and Visualization Interface

- **Description:** Provides a user-friendly web interface with graph-based visualizations showing relationships like zone connectivity, resource distribution, and utility dependencies.

### Alerts and Predictions

- **Description:** Delivers AI-driven notifications about potential risks, such as utility overloads or zoning conflicts, and predictive insights on population growth and resource demand.

## Technologies Used

- **Backend:** Go
- **Database:** Neo4j
- **AI Integration:** OpenAI API
